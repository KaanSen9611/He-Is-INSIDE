**HE IS INSIDE**

Something is inside the building. And you're not supposed to be here.

🏚️ About the Game
He Is Inside is a first-person survival horror game set in an abandoned building where a dangerous creature is waiting for you.

Explore the dark, abandoned environment, stay out of the creature's sight, and search for a way to survive. You won't always have somewhere to run, and hiding might be your only option.

But there's one thing you need to remember:

It doesn't hunt by sound. It hunts by sight.

👁️ Hide. Search. Survive.
Vision-Based Enemy: The creature detects you through its line of sight.
Hide in Lockers: Find a place to hide when the creature gets too close.
Find a Weapon: Search the building and discover a weapon that might turn the tables.
Fight Back: You're not completely defenseless. Defeat the creature to win.
Survival Horror: Explore carefully, make decisions, and try to make it out alive.
How To Hide?: Go near a closet and press "E" want to go out press "E" again
Found The Gun?: Go near it press "E" and now you know what to do :)
🎮 Your Objective
Explore the building, avoid being spotted, find a weapon, and defeat the creature.

Will you survive what's inside?
